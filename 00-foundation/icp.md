# Ideal Customer Profile

**Name and one-line sketch:** Irakli, 20, a KIU student living on campus who regularly uses the shared dormitory washing machines.

**Their situation:** Irakli lives in the KIU campus dormitory and uses the shared washing machines for his laundry. There is currently no way for him to know whether a machine is available without physically going to the laundry area and checking.

**The moment the problem shows up:** Every week when Irakli needs to do laundry, he carries his laundry downstairs to the shared laundry area and finds that all the washing machines are already occupied, not turned on ones are full with washed clothes and left by someone who did not come for them on time, or some machines are simply broken. 

**What it costs them:** He loses time making unnecessary trips to the laundry area, waiting for a machine to become available or for dryer to complete drying, as it sometimes tend to stop itself or not dry clothes at all.

**What they do about it today:** He physically goes to the laundry area to check whether a machine is available. If all machines are occupied, he either waits nearby or takes out left behind clothes from a finished washing machine to not waste his time because of someone's lack of responsibility.

**What they are NOT:**
- KIU students who live off campus and have access to a washing machine at home, because they do not depend on the shared campus washing machines.
- Campus residents who do not use the shared washing machines, because they do not experience the availability problem we are investigating.
