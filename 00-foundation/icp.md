# Ideal Customer Profile

**Name (invented) and one-line sketch:** Luka, 20, a KIU student living on campus who regularly uses the shared dormitory washing machines.

**Their situation:** Luka lives in the KIU campus dormitory and uses the shared washing machines for his laundry. He usually checks machine availability by going to the laundry area in person. Some students also use group chats to ask about availability, but this does not provide guaranteed real-time information.

**The moment the problem shows up:** When Luka needs to do laundry, he carries his clothes downstairs to the shared laundry area and sometimes finds that all the washing machines are already occupied.

**What it costs them:** He loses time making unnecessary trips to the laundry area, waiting for a machine to become available, or carrying his laundry back and returning later.

**What they do about it today:** He checks machine availability in person and sometimes asks other students. If all machines are occupied, he waits, returns later, or tries doing laundry at a quieter time.

**What they are NOT:**
- KIU students who live off campus and have access to a washing machine at home, because they do not depend on the shared campus washing machines.
- Campus residents who do not use the shared washing machines, because they do not regularly experience the availability problem we are investigating.

**Evidence so far:**

**Evidence 1:** One student described using a group chat to ask about washing-machine availability and choosing early hours to avoid busy periods. (Interview Log 01)

**Evidence 2:** A student described waiting approximately 20–30 minutes and having to check machine availability in person. (Interview Log 02)
