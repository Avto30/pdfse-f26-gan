# Ideal Customer Profile

**Name (invented) and one-line sketch:** Luka, 20, a KIU student living on campus who regularly uses the shared dormitory washing machines.

**Their situation:** Luka lives in the KIU campus dormitory and uses shared washing machines. He usually checks availability by going to the laundry area in person. Some residents ask in group chats, but a reliable, universally available real-time status method was not established in our interviews.

**The moment the problem shows up:** When Luka needs to do laundry, he brings his clothes downstairs and sometimes finds that all usable washing machines are occupied, broken, or still holding completed laundry.

**What it costs them:** He may waste time on repeated trips, waiting, or returning later. One Interview 05 participant reported spending around an hour finding a free machine; experiences vary.

**What they do about it today:** He checks availability in person, sometimes asks other students, waits, returns later, moves unattended loads carefully, or tries a quieter time.

**What they are NOT:**
- KIU students who live off campus and do not rely on campus shared washing machines.
- Campus residents who never use these facilities and have no need to find an available shared machine.

**Evidence so far:**

**Evidence 1:** One student described using a dormitory group chat to ask about availability and choosing early hours to avoid busy periods. (Interview Log 01)

**Evidence 2:** A student reported repeatedly checking machine availability in person and waiting around 20–30 minutes for unattended laundry to be collected. (Interview Log 02)

**Evidence 3:** Interview 05 described taking up to approximately an hour to find an available machine, while Interview 06 described repeatedly going up and down and sometimes waiting 10–15 minutes before moving unattended laundry. (Interview Logs 05, 06)
