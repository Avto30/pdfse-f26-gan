# Interview 06 · KIU Student, Dorm F · Sandro (interview was done in English)

**Week:** 3 · **Asker:** G.P. · **Logger:** N.J. & A.G. · **Consent:** yes; notes and recordings. · **Real or practice:** real.

**Context:** KIU student living in the dormitories and regularly using the shared laundry facilities. His main difficulty is the time required to find available washing machines, wait for other students, and collect others left behind clothes.

**Quotes**:

- "I usually do laundry every 2 week, mostly on weekends, as there are less people on campus."
- "There are many times when I have to go up and down to look for free washing machines or wait for them to end and that of course takes a lot of time."
- "Roughly, I wait for 10-15 minutes until someone comes for their left behind clothes, and if they do not I have to put them out myself to not waste any more time because of someone else."
- "Actually there was one time when my own clothes were removed by someone else, because I had some private issues and could not get them on time and I went in the laundry room 15-20 minutes late and found half of my clothes dropped on the floor, which of course needed another wash afterwards. "
- "Of course I am not happy about touching someone's clothes and moving them myself, but what can we do, some people just do not leave u any other choice."
- "There was 2 or 3 times when I could not make it in time and one time I told u what happened but on other 2, they had just left my clothes on the chair which is completely okay, as I do not think that someone should wait for me to come down and get my clothes for a long time, no matter the reason, so yea as long as it is done properly I do not have any problems with it."
- "Somehow knowing whether machines are available or not would help so much as that is major problem why my time is wasted and I would save much time knowing when to go down. "

**Workaround:** Visits the laundry room in the evenings, checks machine availability manually, and sets a timer to collect his clothes as soon as possible. If someone leaves their washed clothes in a machine, he usually waits 10–15 minutes before moving them to a chair. 

**Cost:** Time lost waiting for machines and repeatedly checking the laundry room. Moving other students' clothes may also create uncomfortable situations.

**Frequency:** He does laundry every 2 week, and problem tends to appear once in every 2 washes.

**Specific incident:** Someone was waiting for their clothes to be washed while having 2 full buckets of others and therefore needed one single washing machine for themselves for a whole day and  Sandros clothes were once put out from washing machine, as he could not go there in time becauyse of some problems and half of his clothes were on the floor.


**Prediction check (post-interview clarification):** This interview provides evidence that unattended completed laundry causes waiting and inconvenience, but it does not establish how long owners typically take to collect their laundry after a cycle ends. The original 15-minute falsification condition remains unverified.

**Surprise ★:** The problem is not only the availability of machines. Students also leave clothes unattended, some machines are broken and some student tend to not respect others clothes or take into account that other students need to use a washing machine too and therefore not take it for yourself for a whole day.

**Commitment:** : Introduced 2 of his friends who also tend to use the laundry room very often and may have useful insights.
