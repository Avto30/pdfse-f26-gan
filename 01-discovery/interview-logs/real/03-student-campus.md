**Workaround:** The student used to do laundry early in the morning, check machine availability in person, and move unattended clothes to dryers. Due to repeated problems, they now take their laundry to Tbilisi. 
**Cost:** Waiting up to 30 minutes, lost study time, stress, and additional work caused by malfunctioning machines.  
**Frequency:** Previously used the facilities frequently but has not used them this year. Waiting and technical problems were recurring issues.  
**Prediction check:**  Unattended laundry was discussed, but whether it was mentioned before we asked is unconfirmed. The 15-minute prediction remains unverified.  
**Surprise ★:** The student stopped using the campus laundry facilities entirely. Technical problems were a greater concern than expected.
