# Interview 04 · KIU Student G building, Campus Laundry ·  Tekla

**Week:** 3 · **Asker:** AG · **Logger:** GP, NJ · **Consent:** yes; notes and recording
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** KIU student who uses the shared campus washing machines approximately once a week. We interviewed her to understand her actual experience with campus laundry facilities.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if completed laundry is usually picked up within 15 minutes after the washing cycle ends.

**Workaround:** Does not currently have a good workaround, has had to dry her clothes in her room multiple times. Her ex-roommate used to wash everything by hand, which is a potential workaround to the washing machine availability problem. 
**Cost:** Has to go down multiple times just to check and loses time, even then some people leave their clothes and you have to wait. There are also times when the machine shows one time and takes a greater time to actually finish.
**Frequency:** She washes her clothes every week. She frequently encounters occupied washing machines, especially in Building G.  
**Prediction check:**  Mentioned multiple times when she had to wait more than 10 minutes and nobody showed up.  
**Surprise ★:**  She had an incident when she literally could not get back all of the clothes that she took to clean and has not found them since. She did contact people and she told us about it but we decided to leave that out of the quotes.
**Commitment:**  Offered herself up to more meetings if necessary, also suggested some changes that should be made in her opinion.
**Next:**  Will consider washing clothes by hand and drying them to be a viable workaround to our problem and will ask later interviewees.
