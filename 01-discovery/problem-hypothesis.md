Copy to `01-discovery/problem-hypothesis.md`. Commit it **before your first real interview**. The commit date is your proof. Finished example: `examples/sakhli-problem-hypothesis.md`.

## Step 1: What each of us believes (2 minutes, silently, then share)
| Teammate | What I believe the real problem is |
|---|---|
| Avtandili Gogoladze | There are too many students and not enough washing-machines to acomodate them. |
| Nita Javelidze | Students waste effort and time because they do not know the current availability of the shared washing machines. | 
| Gigi Peikrishvili | Clean laundry is not picked up on time, clogging the overall rotation of students trying to use washing-machines. |
## Step 2: The hypothesis we test first
> **We believe** Luka, 20, a KIU student living on campus who regularly uses the shared dormitory washing machines,
> **struggle with** finding washing machines occupied by completed laundry that has not been picked up
> **when** they bring their laundry to the shared laundry area and find that completed laundry is still inside the washing machines,
> **which costs them** time waiting for a washing machine to become available or returning later.
> **Today they** wait for the owner to collect their laundry, return later, or sometimes remove the completed laundry from the machine.
> **We are wrong if** completed laundry is usually picked up within 15 minutes after the washing cycle ends
> 
## Step 3: The beliefs we did not pick
Each one becomes at least one question in your interview script, so you still hear about it.
- [belief] → script question [number]

- Not knowing the current availability of the washing machines wastes students' time and effort (Nita) → script question
