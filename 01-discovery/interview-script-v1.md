# Interview Script v1

**Problem we are investigating:** Campus washing-machine availability
**Our prediction:** Completed laundry is usually picked up within 15 minutes after the washing cycle ends
**Language:** Georgian/English

## Opening (2 min, word for word)
Hello, we are learning how campus students use shared washing machines. We are here to understand your experience, not to sell anything and we have no product to show. Is it okay if my teammate takes notes? We won't use your name.

## Warm-up (2 min)
1. What is your name, which faculty are you in, and how long have you been living on campus?
2. How would you describe your experience of living on campus?

## Core (12 to 15 min):
1. How often do you wash/dry your clothes on campus?
2. Do you know how to use the washing machines/dryers correctly and efficiently?
3. What do you think you don't know? (Optional)
4. Tell me about the last time you took your laundry to be washed/dried.
5. Walk us through your typical experience using these laundry facilities.
6. Are you satisfied with using this equipment?
7. Why are you satisfied/dissatisfied? Describe in detail the process or experience that led you to feel this way.
8. What were the consequences of this experience? What benefits did you gain? / What problems did you encounter?
9. How do you currently deal with this problem? / What would you improve or wish was better?
10. Have you tried doing anything to fix or improve the situation?
11. Have you ever encountered a situation where someone's laundry was left unattended? How often has this happened? What did you do in that situation?
12. Do you think the action you took was appropriate?
13. Hypothesis Testing:
    1. Have you ever encountered a situation where all washing machines/dryers were occupied? If so, how many times has this happened?
    2. Do you have any way of finding out whether a washing machine/dryer is available?
14. In your opinion, which part of this process takes the most time? For example, bringing your laundry downstairs, waiting for others, restarting a washing cycle because the clothes were not washed properly, or dealing with a cycle that takes longer than the time initially displayed.


## Close (2 min)
1. Who else should we talk to about this topic?
2. If we have any updates, would it be okay to contact you again?

## Follow-up bank
- And then what happened? · Why was that a problem? · When was the last time that happened?
- Can you show me? · What would that let you do that you can't now? · Silence: count to three.
