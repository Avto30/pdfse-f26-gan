# Interview Script v2

**Problem we are investigating:** Campus washing-machine availability
**Our prediction:** Completed laundry is usually picked up within 15 minutes after the washing cycle ends
**Language:** Georgian/English

## Opening (2 min, word for word)
Hello, we are learning how campus students use shared washing machines. We are here to understand your experience, not to sell anything and we have no product to show. Is it okay if my teammate takes notes? We won't use your name.

## Warm-up (2 min)
1. What is your name, which faculty are you in, and how long have you been living on campus?
2. How would you describe your experience of living on campus?

## Core (12 to 15 min):
1. How often do you wash/dry your clothes on campus?
2. Tell me how you usually choose a washing/drying program and decide how to use the machine. Was there a time you were unsure what to do?
3. What do you think you don't know? If Q2 reveals uncertainty: What specifically were you unsure about the last time?
4. Tell me about the last time you took your laundry to be washed/dried.
5. Walk us through your typical experience using these laundry facilities.
6. Think of your most recent laundry session. Which parts went smoothly and which, if any, were difficult?
7. Why are you satisfied/dissatisfied? Describe in detail the process or experience that led you to feel this way. Can you describe a specific occasion that made you feel that way?
8. What were the consequences of this experience? What benefits did you gain? / What problems did you encounter?
9. How did you deal with this problem the last time it happened? What did you try, and what happened?
10. Have you tried doing anything to fix or improve the situation? What did you try, and what happened?
11. Have you ever encountered a situation where someone's laundry was left unattended? When was the last time this happened, and what did you do?
    *Follow-up: The last time you noticed finished laundry left in a machine, approximately how long did it stay there? How did you know the washing cycle had finished?*
12. What happened after you took that action? How did the other people involved respond, if at all?
13. Hypothesis Testing:
    1. Have you ever encountered a situation where all washing machines/dryers were occupied? If so, how many times has this happened? When was the last time? How long did you wait?
    2. Do you have any way of finding out whether a washing machine/dryer is available? How did you check availability the last time you needed one?
14. During your last laundry session, which part of the process took the most time? Approximately how long did it take?

Prefer asking without examples first, but if necessary: For example, bringing your laundry downstairs, waiting for others, restarting a washing cycle because the clothes were not washed properly, or dealing with a cycle that takes longer than the time initially displayed.


## Close (2 min)
1. Who else should we talk to about this topic?
2. If we have any updates, would it be okay to contact you again?

## Follow-up bank
- And then what happened? · Why was that a problem? · When was the last time that happened?
- Can you show me? · What would that let you do that you can't now? · Silence: count to three.
