# Problem Statement

KIU students living in dormitories struggle with shared laundry facilities because washing machines are often occupied, malfunctioning, or filled with clothes that other students have not collected (Interview Logs 01, 02, 03, 04).

When students arrive at the laundry room, they may have to wait for an available machine, repeatedly check its availability, or move someone else's laundry to start their own washing (Interview Logs 01, 02, 03, 04).

These difficulties waste students' time, sometimes requiring 10–30 minutes of waiting, and can interfere with their academic and personal responsibilities (Interview Logs 01, 02, 03).

Moving unattended laundry also creates risks and uncomfortable situations, including clothes shrinking after being transferred into a dryer and personal belongings going missing (Interview Logs 02, 04).
Students try to manage these problems by visiting the laundry room at quieter hours, checking machine availability in person or through group chats, and removing unattended clothes, but these workarounds do not consistently prevent delays and inconvenience (Interview Logs 01, 02, 03, 04).
