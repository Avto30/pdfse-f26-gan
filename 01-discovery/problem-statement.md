# Problem Statement

KIU students living in dormitories struggle with shared laundry facilities because washing machines can be occupied, malfunctioning, or filled with clothes that other students have not collected (Interview Logs 01, 02, 03, 04).

When students arrive at the laundry room, they may have to wait for an available machine, repeatedly check its availability, or move someone else's laundry to start their own washing (Interview Logs 01, 02, 03, 04).

These difficulties waste students' time, sometimes requiring 10–30 minutes of waiting, and can interfere with their academic and personal responsibilities (Interview Logs 01, 02, 03).

Laundry-related problems can also lead to damaged or missing belongings: one student described clothes shrinking in a dryer, while another described clothes going missing after someone moved unattended laundry (Interview Logs 02, 04).

Students try to manage these problems by checking availability in person or through group chats, doing laundry at quieter hours, moving unattended clothes, or even taking their laundry home, but these workarounds still cost time and effort (Interview Logs 01, 02, 03, 04).
