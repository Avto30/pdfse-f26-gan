# Problem Statement

KIU students living in the dormitories experience difficulties using the shared laundry facilities, especially when washing machines and dryers are occupied, broken, or left with other students' clothes inside (Interview Log 01). The problem occurs on "laundry days", when students must repeatedly check machine availability, wait for others to collect their clothes, or return to the laundry room several times (Interview Log 01).

This wastes students' time and interrupts their academic work and other responsibilities (Interview Log 01). Students may also face uncomfortable situations when moving other people's clothes out of machines, while transferring clothes into dryers can risk damaging or shrinking them (Interview Log 01). The lack of a way to check machine availability remotely forces students to manage the process manually and makes doing laundry more frustrating and time-consuming (Interview Log 01).
