# Problem Statement

KIU students living in dormitories struggle with shared laundry facilities because washing machines can be unavailable, malfunctioning, or occupied by clothes that other students have not collected (Interview Logs 01, 02, 04, 05, 06).

When students need to do laundry, they may repeatedly check machine availability, wait for an available machine, or move someone else's clothes, with one student reporting that finding a free machine can take approximately an hour (Interview Logs 01, 02, 05, 06).

These difficulties waste students' time, disrupt their schedules, and can even lead them to stay awake late, wake up early, or take their laundry away from campus (Interview Logs 01, 03, 05).

Moving unattended laundry can also create uncomfortable situations and risks to personal belongings, including damaged clothing, missing items, and clothes being left on the floor (Interview Logs 02, 04, 06).

Students currently deal with these problems by checking availability in person or through group chats, using timers, visiting at quieter hours, waiting, or moving unattended clothes, but these workarounds do not consistently make laundry convenient or predictable (Interview Logs 01, 02, 03, 05, 06).
