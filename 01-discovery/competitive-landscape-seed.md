## The strongest incumbent

**Physically going to the laundry room to check machine availability.**

All three interviewees describe checking machines in person as a way to determine availability, making it one of the most established behaviors in the current laundry process (01, 02, 03). Although this method causes repeated trips and wasted time, students continue relying on it because they do not consistently have dependable availability information before reaching the laundry room (01, 02, 03).
