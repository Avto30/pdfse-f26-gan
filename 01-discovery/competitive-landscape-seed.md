## The strongest incumbent

**Physically going to the laundry room to check machine availability.**

All four interviews mention the need to inspect the laundry room or make trips to find a usable machine (01, 02, 03, 04). Even though that can waste time or end with all machines occupied, the interviewees repeatedly describe doing it in practice, while the chat mentioned by Interview 01 is not a reliably established option for everyone (01, 02, 03, 04).

## Alternatives nobody mentioned

- No commercial laundry-availability app or reservation service was named in Interviews 01–04. This does **not** prove such services do not exist; they were simply not mentioned.
- No online-researched competitors have been added to the evidence table.
