## 2. Pattern journal, Weeks 1 to 4

### Week 1 · Act like an owner, not a role

During the first week, we formed our team and started organizing our GitHub repository. My main role was Build, which meant I would be responsible for the technical side of our product.

I worked on the repository structure and README so we could keep our files organized and make it easier for everyone to work together. I also participated in our early discussions about possible problems.
Even though my main responsibility was supposed to be building the product, I knew I couldn't just wait for the others to decide what we were making. I wanted to understand the problem from the beginning as well.

My contributions: [README](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/blob/main/README.md), [Team Contract](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/blob/main/00-foundation/team-contract.md)

### Week 2 · Evidence over opinion

This week, we had to choose which problem our team would investigate. I worked on the Four Filters Scorecard, where we compared our ideas instead of simply choosing the one we liked most.

I also helped prepare the ICP, Problem Hypothesis, and Interview Script files. My initial opinion was that there were too many students and not enough working washing machines in the dormitories.
We eventually decided to focus our first hypothesis on clothes being left inside machines after washing had finished. Even though this wasn't exactly my original idea, I agreed that we should test it through interviews and see what students actually experienced.

I also added our first decision to DECISIONS.md so we had a record of what we had chosen and why.

My contributions: [Four Filters Scorecard](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/blob/main/00-foundation/four-filters-scorecard.md), [Problem Hypothesis](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/blob/main/01-discovery/problem-hypothesis.md), [DECISIONS.md](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/blob/main/DECISIONS.md)

### Week 3 · Kill your favorite idea

This week, I continued working on our interview questions and problem hypothesis. I revised the interview script as our understanding of the problem changed and helped prepare the second version.

I also worked on the Outreach Tracker and helped organize interviews with students. Preparing the interviews made me realize that asking the right questions was just as important as collecting answers.
At first, I was mostly thinking about the number of available machines, but our questions needed to cover other problems too, including unattended laundry, waiting times, and machine failures.

My contributions: [Interview Script v1](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/blob/main/01-discovery/interview-script-v1.md), [Interview Script v2](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/blob/main/01-discovery/interview-script-v2.md), [Outreach Tracker](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/blob/main/01-discovery/outreach-tracker.md), [Problem Hypothesis](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/blob/main/01-discovery/problem-hypothesis.md)

### Week 4 · The prototype is the conversation

This week, I spent time working on our interview logs. I helped prepare and organize our practice interview log and uploaded a real interview I conducted with 2 students living in Building G.

During both interviews, the students talked about broken washing machines, long cycles, and having to go downstairs several times to check whether a machine was free.
The most surprising part for me was one of the student's experience with someone moving her clothes after she was only five minutes late. Some of her clothes went missing, and she still hadn't found them. That showed us that the problem wasn't only about waiting for a machine.
I uploaded the interview logs to GitHub. Even though my main role is Build, I ended up spending a lot of time on interviews and Discovery. I think understanding these problems properly will help us avoid building something that students don't actually need.

My contributions: [Interview Logs (04)](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/blob/main/01-discovery/interview-logs/real/04-student-campus.md), [Interview Logs (05)](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/blob/main/01-discovery/interview-logs/real/05-student-campus.md), [Practice Interview](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/tree/main/01-discovery/interview-logs/practice), [Interview Script v2](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/blob/main/01-discovery/interview-script-v2.md), [Outreach Tracker](https://github.com/Avto30/pdfse-f26-gan/blob/main/01-discovery/outreach-tracker.md)
